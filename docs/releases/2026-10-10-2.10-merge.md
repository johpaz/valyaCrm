## What shipped
- `POST /api/v1/crm/oportunidades/:id/ganada` with `{ valorCierre,
  fechaCierreReal?, comentario? }`: the only way to mark a deal won, used by
  the app and by the agent's `crear_venta_ganada`. Records exactly one
  `VentaGanada` (unique `oportunidadId`) with the amount, the actual close date
  (a Bogotá calendar day; default today; not future, not before the creation
  day) and the comment (max 500). Sets `estado`, `valorCierre` and
  `fechaCierreReal`; `fechaCierre` stays the expected date. 409 if already won;
  repairs an interrupted earlier attempt; returns the 2.2 detail shape.
- `PATCH …/estado` (2.3) refuses "Cerrado Ganado"; moving a won deal to another
  stage removes its sale and clears `valorCierre` and `fechaCierreReal`.
- The 2.1 list and 2.2 detail now carry `valorCierre` and `fechaCierreReal`.
- Agent: `crear_venta_ganada` uses the shared action (ignores the agent's
  `vendedorId`, refuses a text `valor`); `actualizar_oportunidad` cannot set
  "Cerrado Ganado" and removes the sale when reopening.
- 39 new tests (`bun test`: 80 of 80).

## Known issues and tech debt
- Business time is Bogotá: a rep in another time zone may not be able to pick
  their own "today" until Bogotá's day changes.
- Reopening reads, then updates, without a transaction; a win at that exact
  instant could leave a sale on a deal that isn't won. The next win attempt
  repairs it.
- No existing won deals lacked a sale in `crm_vendedor_b2b`; recount if
  production uses another database.
