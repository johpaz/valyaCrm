## What shipped
`PATCH /api/v1/crm/actividades/:id/estado` with `{ estado }` (JSON only): one
of the six exact statuses; entering "Finalizada" sets `fechaFinReal`, leaving
it removes it, repeating it keeps the original; returns the activity in the
board-list shape (3.1). A `pre('findOneAndUpdate')` hook on Actividad refreshes
`fechaActualizacion` on every update, as 2.3 did for deals.

## Known issues and tech debt
- The status is read then written; two changes at the same instant could
  leave `fechaFinReal` out of step.
- The deal stage change (2.3) still accepts a form-encoded body; 3.2 accepts
  JSON only.
- 6.6 must use `cambiarEstadoActividad`, not `editarActividad`, or finishing
  from WhatsApp won't record `fechaFinReal`.
